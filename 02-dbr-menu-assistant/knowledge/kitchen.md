# Dum Biryani Rasoi kitchen details

Copied from dumbiryanirasoi.in (home, menu and meal plans pages) on 19 Nov 2026. Each section below becomes one chunk in the search index.

## Hours

The kitchen is open every day for lunch from 12 PM to 4 PM and for dinner from 7 PM to 1 AM. Outside these hours the kitchen is closed and can't take orders.

## Address and delivery area

The kitchen is at 58 D-6, SVP Nagar, Four Bungalows, Hanuman Mandir Lane, Andheri West, Mumbai 400053. It's a cloud kitchen for delivery.

Direct delivery covers up to 5 km from Four Bungalows, subject to address confirmation. Areas served include Andheri West, Versova, Four Bungalows, Lokhandwala, Oshiwara, DN Nagar, Veera Desai Road and Yari Road. The delivery charge for direct orders is quoted for each address before payment.

## How to order

Customers can order on Zomato, Swiggy or Magicpin. The app handles checkout, payment and tracking. App prices, fees and availability may differ from the website.

For a direct order, customers send their selection to the kitchen on WhatsApp at +91 91374 61761, or call the same number. Before taking payment, the kitchen confirms availability, the delivery charge and the final total, since the website takes no payment itself.

## Prices on the menu

The menu prices are direct-order prices. Prices on Zomato, Swiggy and Magicpin may be different.

## Bulk orders and pre-orders

Biryani by the kilogram (1 kg serves 4 to 5) is for pre-order only and must be arranged in advance through a direct order. Custom gravies by the kilogram are quoted on WhatsApp for each group order.

## Meal plans

Prepaid thali meal plans deliver to one regular address within 5 km, at a fixed lunch or dinner slot. The price of a plan covers food only, and delivery is quoted separately. Customers send their area, start date and slot on WhatsApp, and the kitchen confirms coverage, the menu and any delivery charge before payment.

- 5-Meal Starter: veg thalis ₹579 (₹115.80 a meal) or chicken thalis ₹719 (₹143.80 a meal). Runs on consecutive selected days.
- 22-Meal Work Month: veg thalis ₹2,499 (₹113.59 a meal) or chicken thalis ₹3,099 (₹140.86 a meal). Includes 2 pause credits.
- 26-Meal Full Month: veg thalis ₹2,899 (₹111.50 a meal) or chicken thalis ₹3,599 (₹138.42 a meal). Includes 3 pause credits.

A pause must be requested at least 24 hours ahead. Customers can order one thali at the regular price as a paid trial before choosing a plan.

## Sample meal plan week

The website shows an example week, and the kitchen agrees on the dishes with each customer before booking. Veg days rotate the Deluxe Veg Thali, Chole Rice, Rajma with 4 Chapatis, Rajma Rice and Chole with 4 Chapatis. Chicken days rotate the Deluxe Chicken Thali, Chicken Masala with Rice and Chicken Masala with 4 Chapatis. Deluxe thalis include dal, rice and three chapatis.

## Office and team orders

For 10 or more meals to one address, the kitchen quotes a rotating team menu for the headcount, delivery time and dietary mix. Veg, egg and non-veg meals can be combined in one recurring office order. Approved business accounts can ask for GST invoices and monthly billing.

## Allergies and ingredients

The website does not list every allergen. Customers with an allergy or ingredient concern should ask the kitchen directly on WhatsApp or by phone before ordering.

## Spice levels

Each dish has a spice level from 0 to 3. Level 0 isn't spicy, 1 is mild, 2 is medium and 3 is hot.

Some food images on the website are AI-generated serving illustrations. Actual dishes and portions may differ from the images.
